Design exploration · 2026

Three directions

Each prototype reimagines the homepage of the AI resume site with a different identity. Open each one to compare hero treatment, ambient styling, and the chat side panel. Pick the direction you want — or mix and match.

Variant A

Conversational

Lean into the AI identity. Inline "Ask me anything" prompt in the hero, ambient cursor-follow glow, streaming chat with a blinking caret and clickable evidence chips, animated AI orb on the right.

Variant B

Editorial

Magazine-style asymmetric hero with a large serif monogram, dot-grid background, italic pull quote, dual primary/secondary CTAs (chat + PDF), numbered sections, traffic-light fit verdict.

Variant C

Engineer

Command-palette hero with prominent ⌘K, code-fenced tagline, real SVG architecture diagram in How-It-Works, CLI-styled fit assessment, terminal-feel chat panel with mono streaming.

Prototypes are static HTML (Tailwind CDN + Google Fonts). The real site lives in src/ and is untouched.
